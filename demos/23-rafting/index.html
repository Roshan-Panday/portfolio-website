Things to carry while Rafting

  • Quick-drying shorts or swimming costume, and T-shirt.
  • Sunglasses, Sunscreen Lotion, and Antiseptic Cream.
  • Extra Undergarments and Towel.
  • Swimming Goggles, Sneakers, or Floaters.
  • Plastic Bags for wet things.
  • Must Keep Waterproof Bag for Camera and other devices.
  • Energy Drinks / Energy Bars in Zip Lock Waterproof Bags.
  • Must Carry Woolen clothes during winters.
  • Use mosquito repellents and lotions during camping.

Rafting Points Rishikesh

Brahmapuri to NIM Beach

Main Rapids : Body Surfing, Initiation, Double Trouble. Rafting Distance : 9 Kms

Clubhouse to NIM Beach

Main Rapids : Clubhouse, Butterfly, Body Surfing, Initiation and Double Trouble. Rafting Distance : 12 Kms

Shivpuri to NIM Beach

Main Rapids : Return to Sender, Roller Coaster, Golf Course, Club House, Initiation, Body Surfing, Double Trouble.

Marine Drive to NIM Beach

Main Rapids : Three Blind Mice, Black Money, Cross Fire, Return to Sender, Roller Coaster, Golf Course, Club House, Initiation, Body Surfing,  Double Trouble. Rafting Distance : 24 Kms
